The first beta: a Dishonored seed can be played start to finish with the in-game module, with no manual `/check`s.

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `dishonored.apworld` | The Archipelago world. Double-click it, or put it in Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder. |
| `dishonored-archipelago-0.9.0-dll.zip` | The in-game module. Extract it into the game's `Binaries\Win32` folder, next to `Dishonored.exe`. |
| `dishonored-archipelago-0.9.0-modloader.zip` | The same module for players who use [Dishonored Mod Loader](https://www.nexusmods.com/dishonored/mods/383). Extract it into `Binaries\Win32` and keep the loader's own `dinput8.dll`. |

Install only one of the two zips. Then open **Dishonored Client** from the Archipelago Launcher and start the game.

## What it does

- **Items:** received powers, Piero's upgrades, equipment and bone charm slots are granted in-game. Items you haven't received can't be bought, aren't handed out by scripts, and are taken away from old saves. Unreceived ammo stays at 0, and the sword is kept out of your hands until Progressive Sword arrives.
- **Checks:** all 100 locations are detected automatically, at low and high chaos, including mission victories and the goal (the ending).
- **Zone keys:**
  - A door into an area whose key you haven't received is refused.
  - If the story or a save puts you in such an area, you're warned and sent back to the main menu after 6 seconds, unless you walk out.
- **Messages:** items sent and received, and Archipelago chat, are shown in-game.

## Options

- `void_walkers_arsenal` (on by default): turn it off if you don't own the Void Walker's Arsenal DLC. You then get 6 bone charm slot items instead of 10.
- `starting_zone_keys`: `none` (default), `prison`, or `intro`.
- `death_link`.

## Requirements and known issues

- Tested on the Steam version of Dishonored, in English. Other builds (GOG, Epic) and languages are untested; the module's log says on startup if your build differs.
- Some checks need specific choices; see the game's info page:
  - poison Granny Rags's elixir still;
  - do at least one of Slackjaw's jobs;
  - spare one Boyle sister and fight Lord Shaw's duel.
- A power received before the Void has no visible mana bar until the Outsider gives you the mark.
- Loading a save inside the Void can take Blink away there.
- Upgrades removed from an old save may keep their effect.
- If a check isn't detected, send it with `/check <location name>` and report it.

## Reporting a problem

Please include `archipelago_native.log`. It's in `Binaries\Win32` (or `Binaries\Win32\NativeMods` with the mod loader, or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DishonoredArchipelago`). Also say what you did just before the problem. Pressing F8 in-game writes a marker into the log at that moment.
