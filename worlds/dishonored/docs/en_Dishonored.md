# Dishonored

Logic built off the Manual created by greenranger1200.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Your powers, sword, equipment, equipment upgrades and bone charm slots are shuffled
into the multiworld, along with "zone keys" that control which areas of each mission
you may enter.

## What counts as a location check?

Collecting runes, bone charms, paintings and blueprints, finishing missions, and a
handful of other tasks.

## What is the goal?

Finish *The Light at the End*.

## Choices that keep every check reachable

Some checks only exist on one branch of a mission. To reach them all without
replaying large parts of the game:

- *High Overseer Campbell*: poison the bootleg elixir still for Granny Rags.
- *House of Pleasure*: do at least the first of Slackjaw's two jobs.
- *Lady Boyle's Last Party*: spare at least one of the Boyle sisters, and fight
  the duel with Lord Shaw.

Every other check can be reached whatever you decide.

## Runes, powers and equipment

With the in-game mod installed, received items are granted directly: powers (so you
don't need runes to unlock them), Piero's upgrades and bone charm slots. Until you
receive an item you can't buy it, scripts don't hand it to you, and an old save that
has it loses it (except Blink inside the Void, which you need to cross it). Unreceived ammo and gadgets stay at 0, and the sword is taken out of
your hands until Progressive Sword arrives. "Corvo's Pistol" is the pistol *upgrade*:
Corvo always has the pistol.

## Zone keys

With the in-game mod, a door or boat prompt into an area whose zone key you haven't
received is refused ("Requires the ... Zone Key"). The story also moves you on by itself
(the end of the Prologue takes you to Dunwall Prison); if that puts you in an area
without its key, you're warned and sent back to the main menu after a few seconds,
unless you walk back out first. Load a save from before, or wait for the key.
The prompt that ends a mission is never refused, so a mission's victory never needs the
next area's key.

## Options

- **Void Walker's Arsenal DLC** (`void_walkers_arsenal`, on by default): turn it off if
  you don't own the DLC. Without its bone charm statues the game allows at most 6 bone
  charm slots, so only 6 Bone Charm Slot items are shuffled instead of 10.
- **Starting Zone Keys** (`starting_zone_keys`): `none` (default, like the Manual),
  `prison` (start with the Dunwall Prison key, so finishing the Prologue never sends you
  back to the menu) or `intro` (also the Sewers, Hound Pits Pub and Void keys).
