# Dishonored

## What does randomization do to this game?

Your powers, sword, equipment, equipment upgrades and bone charm slots are shuffled
into the multiworld, along with "zone keys" that control which areas of each mission
you may enter.

## What counts as a location check?

Collecting runes, bone charms, paintings and blueprints, finishing missions, and a
handful of other tasks.

## What is the goal?

Finish *The Light at the End*.

## Choices that keep every check reachable

Some checks only exist on one branch of a mission. To reach them all without
replaying large parts of the game:

- *High Overseer Campbell*: poison the bootleg elixir still for Granny Rags.
- *House of Pleasure*: do at least the first of Slackjaw's two jobs.
- *Lady Boyle's Last Party*: spare at least one of the Boyle sisters, and fight
  the duel with Lord Shaw.

Every other check can be reached whatever you decide.

## Runes and powers

With the in-game mod installed, received powers are granted directly, so you
don't need runes to unlock them.
